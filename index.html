Browser speech, the other option, needs Chrome or Edge — Opera, Brave and Vivaldi lack the speech service key, though everything else works in them. Windows also needs "Online speech recognition" ON under Settings > Privacy & security > Speech. Put START_ATHEL_TERRA.bat beside this file and double-click it.
